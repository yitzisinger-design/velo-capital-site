Straight answers

Frequently asked questions.

What business owners ask before applying — answered without the fine-print runaround.

Will this affect my credit score?

No. Checking your options uses a soft inquiry only, which has zero impact on your score. A hard pull would only ever happen later in the process with a specific lender — and you'd be told before it does.

What are the typical rates?

Rates vary by product, lender, revenue, and credit profile — that's exactly why we shop your file across 100+ partners instead of quoting one number. Your advisor presents every option with the total cost spelled out so you can compare apples to apples.

How quickly can I receive funds?

Most approved businesses are funded within 24–48 hours of completing their application. Timing can vary based on the lender, the product, and how quickly documents come in.

Is this a loan or something else?

It depends on the structure you choose. Some options are traditional loans (term loans, SBA, equipment financing); others, like revenue-based financing or invoice factoring, are advances against future sales or receivables. Your advisor explains exactly how each option works before you commit to anything.

Do I need perfect credit to qualify?

No. Because we underwrite primarily on business revenue and work with a wide partner network, there are real options across a broad range of credit profiles — including owners that traditional banks have turned down.

How much can I qualify for?

Funding ranges from $10K up to $5M depending on your monthly revenue, time in business, and the product. Your options come back with specific amounts after the quick application.

What does your service cost me?

In most cases, nothing directly. We're compensated by the lender when a funding agreement is completed, which is what allows us to provide the matching service at no direct cost to you.

Can I pay off early?

Many of our partner products allow early payoff, and some include early-payoff discounts. Terms vary by lender, so your advisor will flag the prepayment treatment of each option before you sign.
